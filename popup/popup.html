Quick guide

Auto

The default for regular pages. Scrolls, records video, and takes screenshots together. Stops at the bottom or after 30 seconds, then saves.

Manual

For menus, animations, and other interactions you want to record yourself. You control the page until you press Stop.

After Stop, the page scrolls once automatically for screenshots, then returns to your position. The video has already stopped.

Stop and saving

Use Stop on the camera control or Stop recording in this popup. Keep the captured tab active until Saved appears. Saving can take a little while; a failed save shows an error.

Choose a folder

On Windows, choose or create a folder such as Desktop\Site Dossier. Chrome blocks Desktop and Documents themselves. It may ask for folder access again after a browser restart.

What gets saved

A full-page JPEG, video, contact sheet when available, HTML snapshot, and design tokens. Notes and tags are optional. Files stay in your chosen folder.

The camera control stays visible and appears in the JPEG and video. The HTML snapshot is a reference, not a guaranteed offline copy.

Appearance

The popup follows your system theme automatically.
